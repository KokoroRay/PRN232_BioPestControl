Product Background With the rapid development of high-tech agriculture and the growing demand for sustainable farming practices, traditional methods of purchasing agricultural supplies—such as pesticides, fertilizers, and plant protection products—are gradually revealing limitations in terms of accessibility, information transparency, and technical support.Traditional agricultural stores that primarily rely on physical locations often struggle to provide farmers with immediate access to a wide range of biological control products or detailed guidance on how to use them effectively and safely.Today's farmers and agricultural businesses need a shopping experience that allows them to:-View biological pest control products and organic fertilizers anytime, anywhere.-Access detailed technical specifications, ingredients, and usage instructions for each product to ensure crop safety.-Order and pay online conveniently through various methods (COD or Online).-Easily track order status from processing to delivery.-Discover modern, eco-friendly solutions to replace harmful chemical pesticides.On the other hand, business owners and managers in the agricultural sector face challenges in:-Effectively managing a diverse and specialized product catalog (pesticides, fertilizers, bio-agents).-Accurately updating inventory levels and fluctuating prices of seasonal products.-Processing and tracking complex customer orders across different regions.-Managing customer information and purchase history to provide better agricultural consulting.-Generating comprehensive monthly and yearly statistical reports on revenue and top-selling products.Currently, many agricultural supply businesses still rely on fragmented systems such as manual logbooks or simple spreadsheets, which do not adequately support centralized inventory control, multi-role management (Admin, Staff), or real-time sales analysis. Furthermore, there is a lack of integrated platforms that can eventually support advanced features like AI-based crop disease identification.BioPestControl is proposed to address these issues by providing a centralized e-commerce platform specializing in the sale of biological plant protection products. The system is built on a modern tech stack—utilizing ASP.NET Core, SQL Server, and Kafka—to ensure high performance and scalability.The platform allows administrators and staff to efficiently manage products and orders through dedicated Blazor and Razor interfaces, while customers can explore and purchase sustainable agricultural solutions through a user-friendly MVC web interface, promoting a greener and safer future for agriculture.3. Business Opportunity The Vietnamese agricultural sector is undergoing a powerful digital transformation, with a strong shift towards organic and sustainable farming. However, many biological pesticide and fertilizer providers still lack a specialized, high-performance digital sales and management system to reach modern farmers.BioPestControl creates a significant business opportunity by offering:-A specialized e-commerce platform dedicated to biological pest control and plant protection products, filling the gap in the niche "Green Agriculture" market.-Integrated multi-role management, allowing seamless coordination between Admins, Staff, and Customers through optimized interfaces (Blazor, Razor, and MVC).-Advanced technical scalability by utilizing a distributed messaging system (Kafka) and containerization (Docker) to handle high traffic and large-scale data processing.-Real-time business intelligence through comprehensive dashboards for revenue tracking, inventory monitoring, and sales analysis.-Future AI integration potential, specifically for crop disease identification via images, which creates a unique value proposition that standard e-marketplaces cannot provide.With the rise of high-tech farming and the increasing preference for eco-friendly agricultural inputs, building a dedicated online platform allows BioPestControl to:-Increase market reach to farmers and agricultural enterprises across various provinces beyond physical store boundaries.-Improve operational efficiency by automating order workflows, payment processing (COD/Online), and inventory updates.-Build long-term brand authority as a professional and reliable source for biological agricultural solutions.-Enhance customer loyalty through historical data tracking, feedback systems, and personalized product recommendations based on specific crop needs.4. Software Product VisionFor agricultural supply store owners and biological pesticide providers who want a professional and centralized digital distribution system, BioPestControl is a high-performance web-based e-commerce platform that simplifies specialized product management, order processing, and large-scale business analytics.Unlike selling through fragmented social media channels, manual record-keeping, or general-purpose marketplaces that lack technical agricultural depth, BioPestControl provides:-Full Data Ownership & Scalability: Powered by a modern architecture (BLL/DAL) with SQL Server and MongoDB, ensuring data integrity and system stability.-Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Optimized workflows for four distinct roles: Admin (System Control), Staff (Operations), Customer (Shopping), and Guest (Browsing), each with dedicated interfaces (Blazor, Razor, and MVC).-Integrated Advanced Infrastructure: Utilizing Kafka for distributed messaging and Docker/AWS for reliable deployment, ensuring the system can handle high transaction volumes during peak farming seasons.-Specialized Business Monitoring: Real-time tracking of inventory, revenue, and top-selling bio-products to help managers make data-driven decisions.-Future AI-Driven Support: A roadmap to include AI Crop Disease Identification, transforming a simple store into a smart agricultural consultant.Our vision is to digitalize the biological plant protection industry in Vietnam, making eco-friendly farming solutions more accessible, professional, and technologically advanced in the modern agricultural ecosystem.5. Project Scope & Limitations5.1 Major FeaturesFE-01: User Authentication & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Provides a secure           system for registration, login, and password recovery. Implements strict authorization for four   user groups (Admin, Staff, Customer, and Guest) through specialized interfaces: Blazor      for Admin, Razor Pages for Staff, and MVC for Customers.FE-02: Advanced Product & Inventory Management: Facilitates the management of plant protection product categories (pesticides, fertilizers). Admins and Staff can perform CRUD operations, filter products by inventory levels or business status, and manage stock movements (Import/Export).FE-03: Order & Multi-channel Payment: Customers can place orders via a shopping cart or direct purchase. The system integrates popular payment methods such as COD and Online Payment, while supporting order tracking and delivery confirmation.FE-04: Interaction & Feedback Management: Manages two-way communication between customers and the store. Customers can leave comments and product ratings; Admins/Staff have the authority to moderate, respond to, or edit these interactions to improve service quality.FE-05: Real-time Business Analytics & Dashboard: Provides visual statistical charts on revenue, order volume, and best-selling products. This feature enables Admins to quickly grasp the business situation to make informed decisions regarding stock replenishment or promotions.FE-06: Distributed Messaging & Data Storage: Utilizes Kafka for distributed message processing and a hybrid storage approach combining SQL Server (relational data) and MongoDB (unstructured data) to ensure high performance and system scalability.5.2 Limitations & ExclusionsLI-01: Native Mobile Application: The current system focuses on a Responsive Web      platform. It does not yet support Native Apps for iOS or Android and does not function in Offline Mode.LI-02: Automated AI Diagnosis: In the initial phase, AI-based crop disease recognition is considered a future development goal and is not directly integrated into the current version.LI-03: Multi-language & Global Logistics: The system exclusively supports the Vietnamese language and local currency. International shipping services and customs processing are outside the scope of this project.LI-04: Complex Warehouse Automation: While the system supports basic inventory management (In/Out), it does not integrate smart warehouse automation solutions or robotic operations.III. Mô tả hệ thốngBioPestControl is an e-commerce platform specifically designed for stores trading in plant protection products (pesticides, fertilizers, etc.) in Vietnam. The system focuses on providing safe and effective biological pest control solutions, aiming to digitalize traditional business models to strictly manage specialized product categories and complex operational processes.The platform provides a centralized, professional solution that helps:Guest is a user who has not registered or has not logged in to the BioPestControl system. Guests are allowed to access public information about plant protection products, search and filter items, and view community feedback, but are restricted from performing purchasing and transaction-related activities.Customer is a user who has successfully registered and logged in to the BioPestControl system. Customers are authorized to perform full shopping functions, including cart management, payment , maintaining a wishlist, tracking order history, and providing feedback on product quality.Staff  supports direct operational management. Staff members are responsible for managing the product list, processing orders (creating, updating status, or canceling), managing customer information, and engaging with users by responding to, editing, or deleting comments and feedback.Admin has the highest level of administrative authority. In addition to staff-level permissions, the Admin monitors detailed business statistics via a comprehensive Dashboard (total revenue, top-selling products, etc.), manages inventory (Import/Export), manages staff accounts, and oversees the entire system's data integrity.2.
